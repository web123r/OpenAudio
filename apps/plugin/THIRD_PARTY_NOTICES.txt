Transitive dependencies

The libraries above themselves depend on additional crates, all of which are
licensed under one or more of: MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC, BSD-2-Clause, or Zlib.
A full transitive dependency list with license identifiers can be generated
from the workspace with:

  cargo metadata --format-version 1 | \
    jq -r '.packages[] | "\(.name) \(.version)  \(.license)"' | sort

Review the output before redistribution to confirm no copyleft (GPL/LGPL/EUPL)
or proprietary dependencies have been added.
